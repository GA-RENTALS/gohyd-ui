04 · Explore Hyderabad

A city of
two tempos.

From the Old City's stonework to the glass and green of the west, Hyderabad keeps handing you another side to find. Pick a place to open it.

Charminar
Old City

Charminar

Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah raised the Charminar in 1591. After dark, the plaza around it fills with stalls, lights and the smell of hot food.

Jagannathsrs · CC BY-SA 4.0

Laad Bazaar
Old City

Laad Bazaar

The market street beside the Charminar, known for lac bangles, pearls and bridal shopping. Come hungry and ready to bargain.

deadrat · CC BY 4.0

Golconda Fort
West of the city

Golconda Fort

A Qutb Shahi citadel of granite ramparts and ruined halls, famed for the clap-echo near its entrance gate.

Bernard Gagnon · CC BY-SA 3.0

Hussain Sagar
Central Hyderabad

Hussain Sagar

A lake dug in 1563, with a monolithic Buddha statue on an island and the Tank Bund promenade running along its edge.

iMahesh · CC BY-SA 4.0

Cable Bridge
Madhapur · Jubilee Hills

Durgam Cheruvu Cable Bridge

A cable-stayed bridge across Durgam Cheruvu, opened in 2020 and lit up after dark, linking the Jubilee Hills and HITEC City sides of the lake.

iMahesh · CC BY-SA 4.0

HITEC City
The west

HITEC City

The city's tech corridor, where glass towers glow late into the evening and the weekend feels a long way off, until it isn't.

Mohammad Anees Qamar · CC BY 4.0

Shilparamam
Madhapur

Shilparamam

An arts and crafts village of handicraft stalls, open-air artists and colour at every turn.

Nayan j Nath · CC BY-SA 4.0

Ananthagiri
Vikarabad district

Ananthagiri Hills

Forested hills within reach of a day trip, a favourite for trails, birding and a quick escape from the city.

Praveen120 · CC BY-SA 3.0
